# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

US adults 45–70, women and men, landing here from Google Demand Gen ads (YouTube, Discover, Gmail) on an iPhone. They are not gym people. They want to move without pain, keep their balance, sleep better, and feel capable again — without being talked down to. Men in this group need explicit permission that yoga is for them too. They read in bright daylight, one thumb, standing in a kitchen.

## Product Purpose

Second Wind Yoga is an online yoga membership designed specifically for bodies 45 and up: live-feel on-demand classes across gentle, strength & balance, and chair tracks. The landing page has one job — convert an ad click into a started free week. Success = a visitor 45+ trusts the page within 10 seconds and taps "Start my free week".

## Brand Personality

Steady, warm-blooded, plain-spoken. Capability, not youthfulness. The voice of a good physical therapist who happens to be funny — never mystical, never clinical, never condescending.

## Anti-references

- Lululemon-style aspirational-youth fitness marketing (25-year-olds in handstands).
- AARP / healthcare-pamphlet "senior" pastels and stock-photo silver-haired hand-holding.
- The sage-green-on-cream spa cliché every AI yoga page defaults to.
- Woo-woo mysticism (chakra gradients, lotus icons everywhere, Sanskrit as decoration).

## Design Principles

1. **Legibility is the luxury.** 19px+ body, near-AAA contrast, no thin gray text. The design flexes by being effortless to read.
2. **Show bodies like theirs.** Every photo is a real adult 45+, mid-practice, in daylight. No aspirational youth, no frailty framing.
3. **One decision per fold.** Each viewport asks the reader to absorb exactly one idea; the only repeated ask is the free week.
4. **Say the price, say the catch.** This demographic converts on plain talk: real prices, "cancel anytime" in body copy, no countdown timers.
5. **Thumb-first iOS.** 44px+ targets, sticky bottom-of-thought CTAs, native controls (details/summary), safe-area aware, fast on Safari.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum with AAA-level body-text contrast (≥7:1). Base font 19px. Touch targets ≥44px. `prefers-reduced-motion` fully respected. No autoplaying media, no motion-gated content. Works without JavaScript.
